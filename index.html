Privacy Policy — CREASE

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · App version 1.1.0 · Package com.Bravo.crease

CREASE ("the app") is a cricket scoring and team app by Babar Imran ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Playing as a guest (the default)

If you use CREASE without signing in, we collect no account or personal information. Match scores, team rosters, coins, XP, unlocked themes, kits and emblems, and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Ads, described below, are still shown to guests.

Advertising

CREASE shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on main screens, an occasional full-screen ad after a match, an optional video you choose to watch to double a reward, and an ad when you return to the app after a long break. Ads are never shown while you score a ball.

To show and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, and may use them for personalised advertising subject to your consent choices. See Google's advertising policy.

  • Consent: where required (for example the EEA and UK) the app asks for consent with Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time from More → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalisation in Settings → Google → Ads.

If you sign in (Google or email)

Signing in is optional. It is only needed to sync a team across devices, let teammates join with a code, appear in the online players list, or receive team notices. If you sign in, we collect:

  • Account info: the name and email address given by your sign-in provider, and a unique account ID.
  • Team data you enter: team name, team logo or emblem choice, kit choice, and player names on your roster.
  • Presence: your display name, team name and a "last seen" time, shown to other signed-in players so captains can invite them. Signing out removes you from the list.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier used only to deliver team notices your captain posts.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging). It is never sold, and is used only to provide the features above.

On-device features

  • Daily reminder: if you turn it on, the app schedules a local notification on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn it off in More.
  • Scorecard PDF and match cards: created on your phone. They leave your phone only if you choose to share them with another app.

Purchases

The in-app Store uses coins earned by playing. No real money is charged and no payment information is collected.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics that identify you personally.

Children

CREASE is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Play as a guest to avoid any account data collection.
  • Decline personalised ads in the consent form or Android settings.
  • Delete your team at any time from the My Team screen.
  • To delete your account data, email Papajohn3432@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we will update this page and the date above.
