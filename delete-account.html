Delete your CREASE account and data

App: CREASE (com.Bravo.crease) · Developer: Babar Imran

If you only played as a guest

Guest play creates no account. Everything is stored on your phone. To remove it, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → CREASE → Storage → Clear data.

If you signed in

To delete your account and the data linked to it, email us from the address you signed in with:

  1. Send an email to Papajohn3432@gmail.com with the subject "CREASE account deletion".
  2. Include the sign-in email and your display name in the app.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email, account ID)
  • Teams you created: team name, logo and kit choice, and roster
  • Your entry in the online players list and your notification token

What is kept

Nothing linked to you is kept. Ad-related data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google's privacy policy; you can reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Delete only some data

You can delete a team yourself at any time from My Team in the app, and sign out to leave the online players list.
